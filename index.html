About

I am a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, working under the supervision of Dr. Ajay Pratap. My research focuses on IoT-enabled smart healthcare, federated and split learning, fog computing, and machine learning for resource-constrained edge devices.
